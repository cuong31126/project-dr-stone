<p>The island is mostly round in shape, with steep cliff shores and a central mountain, which gives most of the island a 30-45º incline. The island's maximum diameter is under 4km.<span title="Dr. Stone Manga: [[Chapter 133]], Page 5"><sup id="cite_ref-Ch133_Pg5_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ch133_Pg5-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></span> There are a few round craters found on the island, the biggest being on the top of the mountain, possibly indicating volcanic activity. The island is covered in forest, with similar trees to the ones found on the mainland. Jasmine seems to grow abundantly on the island, along with at least one patch of bamboo. Several tiny villages can also be found around the island.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Wavebreak_Cliff">Wavebreak Cliff</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdr-stone.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTreasure_Island%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section" data-testid="log-in-edit-section" aria-label="Sign in to edit"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Wavebreak cliff is the location of two battles. The first is the <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Science" title="Kingdom of Science">Kingdom of Science</a> against Kirisame, Moz and a large number of armed villagers.<span title="Dr. Stone Manga: [[Chapter 126]], Pages 2-3"><sup id="cite_ref-Ch126_Pg2-3_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ch126_Pg2-3-7" aria-label="Reference 7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></span> The second was the decisive battle between Ibara, Senku and <a href="/wiki/Ryusui_Nanami" title="Ryusui Nanami">Ryusui</a>, where they successfully petrify Ibara and obtain the petrification device.<span title="Dr. Stone Manga: [[Chapter 136]], Page 12"><sup id="cite_ref-Ch136_Pg12_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ch136_Pg12-8" aria-label="Reference 8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></span>
</p><p>The cliff itself is almost completely bare, with an approximately 20 meter drop straight into the ocean. Waves are shown crashing high against it. Larger vegetation does not seem to grow along a channel heading toward the mountain, only small grasses and lichen. A few smaller ledges are just over the edge, one of which allowed Ryusui to successfully hide from Ibara and the others with the drone. Part of the cliff seems to have split inwards, creating a sharp 'V' shape.
</p>
