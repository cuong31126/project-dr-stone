## 1. Lo Trinh Cong Nghe Cua Senku (Roadmap of Science)

### Giai doan 1: Khoi phuc su song va Sinh ton (Survival & Resurrection Arc)
- **Nuoc hoi sinh (Revival Fluid - Nital Acid):**
  - Cong thuc: $HNO_3$ (Axit Nitric) + $C_2H_5OH$ (Con Ethanol).
  - Nguyen lieu: Phan doi (Bat guano) ket hop voi ruou len men tu nho dai.
  - Y nghia: Pha vo lop vo hoa da silicat tren co the con nguoi sau 3,700 nam.
  - Cac nguyen to lien quan: C (Cacbon), H (Hydro), N (Nito), O (Oxy).
- **Thuoc no den (Black Powder):**
  - Cong thuc: $KNO_3$ (Kali nitrat - 75%) + S (Luu huynh - 10%) + C (Than cui - 15%).
  - Nguyen lieu: Tinh the muoi nitrat tren tuong hang dong, luu huynh tu suoi nuoc nong, than go dot.
  - Y nghia: Vu khi phong thu dau tien cua Vuong Quoc Khoa Hoc.
  - Cac nguyen to: K (Kali), S (Luu huynh), C (Cacbon), N (Nito), O (Oxy).
- **Xa phong (Soap):**
  - Cong thuc: Muoi kali cua axit beo ($R-COOK$).
  - Nguyen lieu: Tro go (giau Kali cacbonat $K_2CO_3$) + Dau mo dong vat/thuc vat.
  - Y nghia: "Vien da bac si" chong lai vi khuan va nhiem trung trong the gioi da.
  - Cac nguyen to: K, C, H, O.

### Giai doan 2: Che tao Than Duoc Sulfa (Kingdom of Science vs Village Arc)
- **Muc tieu:** Chua benh viem phoi cho Cong chua Ruri cua lang Ishigami.
- **Lo trinh 25 buoc hoa hoc:**
  1. *Luu huynh (S):* Khai thac tu mieng ho axit nui lua chet nguoi.
  2. *Axit Sulfuric ($H_2SO_4$):* "Chua te cua hoa hoc", dung dich an mon cuc manh.
  3. *Axit Clohidric ($HCl$):* Thu tu muoi an va axit sulfuric.
  4. *Natri Hydroxit ($NaOH$ - Xut an da):* Dien phan nuoc muoi ($NaCl + H_2O$).
  5. *Aniline ($C_6H_5NH_2$):* Dieu che tu than da qua cac buoc chưng cat hydrocacbon.
  6. *Thuoc khang sinh Sulfanilamide ($C_6H_8N_2O_2S$):* Tong hop thanh cong than duoc hoa hoc cuu song Ruri.
  - Cac nguyen to cot loi: S (Luu huynh), Cl (Clo), Na (Natri), N (Nito), C, H, O.

### Giai doan 3: Cach mang Nang luong va Dien khi hoa (Power Generation Arc)
- **Dong (Cu):**
  - Khai thac quang Malachite de nung chay thanh dong nguyen chat.
  - Keo thanh soi day dong cuc manh lam cuon cam.
- **Sat (Fe):**
  - Dai cat sat o bo song, nung trong lo cao cung than go va gio thoi thu cong.
  - Re thanh thep va sat de lam loi nam cham dien.
- **Nam cham vinh cuu & May phat dien (Edison Dynamo):**
  - Dung tia set danh vao thanh sat boc day dong de tu hoa thanh nam cham manh.
  - Phat minh ra may phat dien bang tay va sau do la nha may thuy dien mini bang guong nuoc.
- **Thuy tinh & Kinh quang hoc (Glass & Optics):**
  - Thanh phan: Cat thach anh ($SiO_2$) + Voi bot tu vo so ($CaCO_3$) + Tro go.
  - San pham: Binh cau, ong nghiem cho phong thi nghiem hoa hoc va kinh can thi cho Suika.
  - Cac nguyen to: Si (Silic), Ca (Canxi), Na, O.

---

### Giai doan 4: Thap sang Dem toi & Bong den (Light Arc)
- **Vonfram (Tungsten - W):**
  - Kim loai co nhiet do nong chay cao nhat bang tuan hoan (3,422°C).
  - Senku, Chrome va Magma da mao hiem vao hang dong sau de khai thac quang Scheelite.
  - Tro thanh soi dot bong den khong bi chay trong moi truong chan khong.
- **Photpho (P):**
  - Khai thac tu khoang photphat de tao lop phu huynh quang.
  - Cac nguyen to: W (Vonfram), P (Photpho), Si (Silic).

---

### Giai doan 5: Cuoc chien Thong tin & May Truyen Tin (Communications Arc)
- **Bac (Ag):**
  - Dung lam mang rung cho micro truyen am thanh.
- **Tinh the Muoi Rochelle (Rochelle Salt):**
  - Hop chat ap dien ($KNaC_4H_4O_6 \cdot 4H_2O$) bien dao dong co hoc thanh tin hieu dien.
- **Dien thoai di dong / May bo dam (Kingdom of Science Cellphone):**
  - Ket hop ong chan khong, song radio de lien lac qua khoang cach hang chuc km trong chien dich danh bai Tsukasa ma khong can do mau.
  - Cac nguyen to: Ag (Bac), Cu (Dong), W (Vonfram), K (Kali), Na (Natri).

---

### Giai doan 6: Dong co Hoi sinh Vo Han (Treasure Island Arc)
- **Bach kim (Platinum - Pt):**
  - Kim loai quy hiem bac nhat duoc giau trong con tau Soyuz cua Byakuya (cha cua Senku).
  - Tinh chat hoa hoc: Chat xuc tac hoan hao trong phan ung Ostwald.
  - Ung dung: Oxi hoa Amoniac ($NH_3$) thanh Axit Nitric ($HNO_3$) ma khong can phu thuoc vao phan doi tu nhien.
  - Y nghia: Tao ra "Nha may nuoc hoi sinh vo han" de giai cuu toan bo nhan loai tren hon dao kho bau.
  - Cac nguyen to: Pt (Bach kim), N (Nito), H (Hydro), O (Oxy).
